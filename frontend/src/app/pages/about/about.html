About EasyBus

EasyBus is Morocco's modern bus booking platform, making intercity travel simple, fast, and reliable. We connect travelers with bus operators across the country so you can compare schedules, prices, and amenities — all in one place.

Fast Booking

Search, select, and pay in minutes. No queues, no hassle.

Secure Payments

PayPal-protected transactions keep your money safe.

Nationwide Coverage

Routes connecting all major Moroccan cities.

Our Mission

We believe that bus travel should be as easy to book as a flight. Our mission is to digitize Morocco's intercity bus network and provide a seamless experience from search to boarding.

Whether you're commuting between Casablanca and Rabat or exploring the country from Tangier to Agadir, EasyBus is here to make your journey effortless.
